## Keyboard samples

- Source: **Keyboard Soundpack #1 [Typing and Single Keystrokes]**
- Creator: unicaegames
- Original recording: Cherry KC 1000 keyboard with a Shure SM7B
- Source page: https://opengameart.org/content/keyboard-soundpack-1-typing-and-single-keystrokes
- License: CC0 1.0 Universal
- Local files: `keyboard-*` and `space-*`

The local names select fourteen of the source pack's 32 isolated keypress
recordings. SoundEvent volume and pitch parameters provide the keyboard/space
roles; the waveform data is otherwise unchanged.

## Mouse samples

- Source: **Middle Mouse Click**
- Creator: 1j01
- Source page: https://opengameart.org/content/middle-mouse-click
- License: CC0 1.0 Universal
- Local files: `mouse-down.wav`, `mouse-release.wav`

The creator supplied separate physical press and release recordings. They are
kept separate so the DEM edge stream, rather than a timer, controls playback.
